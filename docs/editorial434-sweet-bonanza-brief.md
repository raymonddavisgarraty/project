## Proposed original article structure (editor writes and verifies)

1. Identity and edition — original 2019 title vs similarly named later releases; manufacturer/brand attribution.
2. What players see — 6×5 layout and symbol-count wins; explain the source terminology without inventing a payline count.
3. Free-spin rules and tumbling mechanics — only after first-party manual/rules comparison; distinguish marketing description from certified math.
4. RTP and mathematical variants — report **the approved exact revision/configuration(s)**, not a guessed default. If uncertain, say unavailable. Explain *theoretical* vs *minimum* RTP as distinct labels, not interchangeable metrics.
5. Volatility and outcome variance — do not publish "high" or "medium" until edition-specific conflict resolved; do not infer hit frequency from volatility.
6. Maximum win — resolve stake-basis/cap differences **21,100× vs 21,175×**, with cited documentation and possibly market-specific caveats.
7. What sources establish vs what remains unknown — transparent source attribution and observation dates (only if public disclosure is permitted).
8. Questions and FAQ — game provider, mechanics and RTP variation only where verified; avoid invented comparisons or editorial scores.
9. Related entity navigation — Game→Game IDs must be reviewed before calling any game similar; publisher association requires a verified game/provider claim. No ghost Provider URLs.
10. Responsible use and jurisdiction — neutral information only; commercial offers and Telegram promotion remain disabled without per-market/channel clearance.
